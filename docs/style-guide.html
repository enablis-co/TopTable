Style guide, ink and marker

The mark

A wide bar with round tables beneath it. A room seen from above, which is the app's main screen. Nothing to decode.

Top Table

Stroke weight increases as the mark gets smaller, from 1.8 at 40px to 2.6 at 16px, so the circles hold their shape. Clear space on all sides is the height of the bar.

Never recolour the mark to carry state. It is brand, not interface. Violations use red and amber, the mark stays ink and slate.
Never set the wordmark in anything but Plex Sans 500 with -0.5px tracking. Both T's capitalised, no other case treatment.

Colour

Warm paper rather than cool grey. Warm neutrals keep their character on a projector, where cool greys wash out to a flat nothing.

Colour never carries meaning on its own. Hard violations get a solid 3px left bar, soft get the same bar in amber, pinned guests get a filled dot, tables in violation get a dashed outline. Strip every colour out and the screen still reads. This is not an accessibility footnote, it is the projector requirement.

Type

IBM Plex Sans throughout, two weights. Drawn for technical product work, and its figures are properly designed, which matters when every column in this app is a number that updates as you type.

Severity

The system the whole product turns on. Hard stops a plan publishing. Soft is reported and never blocks. Both are read at a glance from across a room.

There is no success colour. A plan with nothing wrong says so in words. Adding green would mean four colours competing on one panel, and it would imply the plan is finished when it has only passed the rules that exist.
